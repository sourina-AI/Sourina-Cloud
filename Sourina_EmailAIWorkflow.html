01 · Compose

Email Studio

AI email authoring & build

The starting point. Describe the campaign and Email Studio generates a responsive, Outlook-safe HTML email — modular blocks, brand-compliant styling, personalization tokens, subject, preheader and CTA — all clean enough to flow straight into testing and QA.

  • ✓Brief → email in one pass. AI drafts subject, preheader, body copy and CTA from a short campaign brief.
  • ✓Modular, brand-safe blocks. Header, hero, body, CTA and footer built on Outlook-safe tables with dark-mode fallbacks.
  • ✓Personalization tokens. Drops in {{first_name}}, {{product}} and dynamic content blocks.
  • ✓Clean, portable HTML. Exports the exact markup the QA Agent and Narrative Engine consume downstream.

02 · Validate

Narrative Testing Engine

Persona reactions + ready-to-ship rewrite

Before the email ships, it goes on trial. The engine simulates how named Adobe buyer personas react to the exact copy, scores the message honestly, names the competitor wedge you're losing on — then rewrites it ready to ship.

  • ✓11 named personas. CLO, VP L&D, CHRO, L&D Ops, LMS Admin and more — across Decision Makers, Evaluators & End Users.
  • ✓4 honest scores. Resonance · clarity · win probability · future resonance — generic copy scores low, on purpose.
  • ✓Named competitor wedge. Cornerstone, Docebo, Workday Learning, LinkedIn Learning, 360Learning, Absorb, Litmos — and where each beats you.
  • ✓2026 inbox-aware predictions. Open-rate & CTR for email; LinkedIn / Facebook reach for social, tuned to AI-inbox triage.
  • ✓One-shot rewrite. A ship-ready rewrite plus 2 alt variants, downloadable as .txt / .docx, saved to the Narrative Library.

03 · Pre-flight

Email QA Agent

50+ automated checks · launch-readiness score

The final gate. A full-stack QA engine audits the finished HTML across five dimensions, flags every error and warning, and computes a single launch-readiness score so you know — objectively — whether the campaign is cleared to send.

  • ✓HTML & rendering (9). Tag closure, Outlook compatibility, ALT text, image weight, responsive & email-safe CSS.
  • ✓Links (8). CTA verification, HTTPS, approved-link match, tracking params and a required unsubscribe.
  • ✓Stakeholder compliance (8). Required messaging, legal disclaimers, brand tone, CTA placement & offer accuracy.
  • ✓Content & subject line (17). Grammar, tokens, readability, plus subject scoring, spam-trigger detection & 5 alternatives.
  • ✓Launch readiness 0–100. −10 per error, −5 per warning → PASS / WARNING / FAIL, with audit history & JSON export.

How they connect

Every stage hands the next a clean artifact.

The three skills aren't three tools you juggle — they're one pipeline. Output becomes input, and findings loop back to the Studio until the email clears every gate.
