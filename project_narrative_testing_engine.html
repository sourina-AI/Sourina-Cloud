The Problem

Marketers ship copy and hope it lands. The feedback loop is broken.

The friction

Traditional GTM message validation takes weeks — running formal user research, scheduling persona interviews, and consolidating qualitative feedback. By the time signal arrives, the launch window has closed and copy goes out untested. Teams discover their value props confuse buyers after the campaign reports come in.

The bet

What if a marketer could rehearse a campaign against a simulated buyer panel in minutes — get persona reactions, spot confusion flags, surface gaps in the narrative, and iterate before a single dollar is spent? Claude makes that possible. The Narrative Testing Engine is the operational layer on top.

How It Works

Four steps from draft copy to defensible narrative.

The engine accepts pasted copy, a URL, or an uploaded file. From there, it runs a structured persona simulation, scores the narrative against a rubric, and outputs both diagnostic feedback and ready-to-use sales enablement.

STEP 01

Ingest the narrative

Drop in copy, paste a URL, or upload a deck. The engine parses the messaging, value props, and CTAs into a structured representation.

STEP 02

Simulate the panel

Claude role-plays the buyer personas relevant to the product — IT decision-makers, developers, business leaders — each with their own priorities and skepticism.

STEP 03

Surface the flags

The engine outputs confusion flags, messaging gaps, competitive positioning issues, and where personas tune out — graded against a structured rubric.

STEP 04

Generate enablement

From the same narrative, generate sales talk tracks, objection-handling scripts, and revised messaging variants — handing GTM a ready-to-ship asset, not just feedback.
